Linux source-path naming gap

seeds/linux_source_refs.tsv has 127 paths. 125 of them point only at functions that already have names. Two paths still point at FUN_ entries, all TinyXML:

- tinyxml.cpp: 041c34b0, 041c3320, 041c3380
- tinyxml.h: 041c7910, 041c73a0

The checker queue is empty. None of these five pass.

041c34b0 has one signature, TiXmlAttributeSet::Remove, but that signature is also on 041c5c20. The checker requires the signature to belong to one function.
041c3320 and 041c3380 are the two IterateChildren overloads. The checker strips the parameter list, so both collapse to TiXmlNode::IterateChildren and the name is shared.
041c73a0 and 041c7910 are in seeds/ambiguous_signatures.tsv (TiXmlBase::GetChar and TiXmlBase::StringEqual). Their split check found only internal jumps, so they stay unnamed.

24 other FUN_ entries have exactly one assert signature that is shared with another function. They are listed in seeds/shared_signature_funs.tsv. They also fail the unique-signature rule, so they were not added to tools/names_batch_llm.tsv.
